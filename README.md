1. Project Title / Headline
HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard: Identifying What Drives Employee Turnover

2. Short Description / Purpose
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses employee attrition across departments, job roles and demographics. It identifies the factors most associated with employees leaving, so HR teams can make data-driven retention decisions.

3. Tech Stack

Power BI Desktop: dashboard design and interactivity
Power Query: data preparation
DAX: measures (Attrition Rate, Attrition Count, Avg Tenure) and derived bands (Age Group, Income Band, Tenure Band)
Data modelling and visual analytics: segmentation, driver analysis and drill-down

4. Data Source
Source-https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

5. Features / Highlights

Three analytical pages: Overview, Drivers and Employee Details.
Overview: KPI cards for Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Avg Monthly Income and Avg Tenure. Attrition by department and age group, plus gender and environment satisfaction breakdowns.
Attrition drivers: Attrition rate compared across job level, income band, tenure band, business travel, overtime and job role.
Employee-level detail: An age vs monthly income scatter by attrition status, a Work-Life Balance × Job Satisfaction attrition matrix, and a detailed employee table.
Slicers for department and job role.
Engineered bands (age, income, tenure) that turn raw numbers into clear risk segments.
